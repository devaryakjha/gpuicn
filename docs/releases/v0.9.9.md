# gpuicn v0.9.9

Two fixes found while checking v0.9.8 in Matty. The runtime pins are
unchanged: GPUI Kit 0.9.0 on `gpui-pre` 0.3.11 and gpui-icons 0.6.0.

## Fixed

- An `Attachment` image sized with `dimensions` shrinks with a narrow
  column and keeps its aspect ratio, instead of running past the column's
  edge (matty#683). A message's content also lets wide children shrink
  with the row.
- `ShowMore`'s chevron takes the link color; it was drawn invisible.
