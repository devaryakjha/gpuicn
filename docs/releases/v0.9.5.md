# gpuicn v0.9.5

Two more requests from Matty: a clickable author name on messages, and toasts
that tell the app when they leave. Everything is additive; the runtime pins
are unchanged: GPUI Kit 0.9.0 on `gpui-pre` 0.3.11 and gpui-icons 0.6.0.

## Added

- **Message**: the author name can open something, such as a profile card
  (#123).
  - `on_author_click(handler)` draws the name as a link-styled button that
    takes focus with Tab, runs on click, Enter or Space and underlines on
    hover. The handler gets the click and the name's bounds in window
    coordinates, to anchor a popover.
  - `author_element(element)` puts your own element in place of the name,
    such as the `hover_popover` trigger you already wrap around the avatar.
    It inherits the name's weight, size and color.
  - Both work in standard and compact density.
- **Toast**: the app can follow a notification's life (#122).
  - `Notice::on_dismiss(handler)` runs once when a notice starts to leave,
    with a `NoticeDismissal`: `Action`, `Cancel` (such as "Later"), `Close`,
    `Timeout`, `Dismissed` (the app called `dismiss`) or `Replaced` (the app
    pushed the same ID again). `NoticeDismissal::by_reader` is true for Cancel
    and Close. The handler runs after the current update, so it may push
    again.
  - `ToastState::contains(id)` says whether a notice is queued or on screen
    and not already leaving.
  - `ToastState::set_placement(ToastPlacement)` moves the stack to any corner
    or the middle of the top or bottom edge (bottom left stays the default),
    and `set_inset(horizontal, vertical)` sets its distance from the window
    edges, such as to clear a composer.

## Upgrading

```sh
cargo install gpuicn-cli --version 0.9.5 --locked
gpuicn update --to 0.9.5
```

Crate users: `cargo update -p gpuicn`.
