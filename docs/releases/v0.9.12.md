# gpuicn v0.9.12

A Color Picker, for Matty's appearance editor (karyalabs/matty#705). The
runtime pins are unchanged: GPUI Kit 0.9.0 on `gpui-pre` 0.3.11 and
gpui-icons 0.6.0.

## Added

- `color_picker`: `ColorPicker` on a retained `ColorPickerState` picks a
  color with hue, chroma and lightness sliders in OKLCH, plus optional
  `ColorSwatch`es. OKLCH keeps the sliders perceptual: changing the hue keeps
  the color equally light and colorful. `ColorPickerEvent::Change` reports
  every change, and `Oklch` converts to and from sRGB.
- `Slider::gradient`: paints the whole track with color stops in place of
  the filled range, for color sliders.
- `UiText::ColorHue`, `UiText::ColorChroma` and `UiText::ColorLightness`, the
  picker's slider names, translatable like every built-in string.
