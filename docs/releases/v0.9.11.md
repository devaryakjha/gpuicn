# gpuicn v0.9.11

Message row additions for Matty's paper-cut review (karyalabs/matty#679,
karyalabs/matty#680). The runtime pins are unchanged: GPUI Kit 0.9.0 on
`gpui-pre` 0.3.11 and gpui-icons 0.6.0.

## Added

- `Message::on_click`, for opening a message's thread with a click on the
  row, as Mattermost does. It doesn't run for a drag, a double or triple
  click, or a click while text is selected. The row's own controls (the
  gutter, the name, reaction chips and the action bar) keep their clicks, and
  clickable content the caller adds should call `cx.stop_propagation()`.
- `message::reaction_tooltip`: a reaction chip's tooltip with the emoji large
  on top, who reacted under it, and an optional hint such as "(click to add)".
- `message_emoji_action`: a one-click emoji button for `message_actions`, for
  a hover bar's recent reactions.

## Changed

- A reaction chip's plain `Reaction::tooltip` text now shows in the new
  reaction tooltip, with the emoji large on top.
- The gutter, the name and reaction chips stop their clicks from reaching the
  row.
