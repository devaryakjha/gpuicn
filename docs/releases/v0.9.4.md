# gpuicn v0.9.4

Six requests from Matty: presence dots on avatars, menus that fit the window,
hover profile cards, quoted posts, accessible-name audits and sortable lists.
Everything is additive; the runtime pins are unchanged: GPUI Kit 0.9.0 on
`gpui-pre` 0.3.11 and gpui-icons 0.6.0.

## Added

- **Avatar**: `status(AvatarStatus)` draws a presence dot over the
  bottom-right corner, sized to the avatar (8, 10 or 12px), in a ring of the
  surface color (#115).
  - Online, Away and Dnd use the `success`, `warning` and `destructive`
    tokens; Offline is a hollow ring; `Custom { color, label }` covers the
    rest. Set the tokens in your theme to restyle every dot.
  - `ring_color` matches the ring to the surface, such as `colors.sidebar`.
  - The status joins the accessible name: "Ana Lopez, away".
- **Popover**: `hover_popover(id, label)` for profile cards (#118).
  - The trigger is one focusable button named `label`, usable on an avatar
    or an inline name; `trigger_style_with` restyles it.
  - Resting the pointer opens it after 500ms without moving focus, and the
    pointer can move into the card; it closes 300ms after the pointer leaves
    (`open_on_hover` sets both delays).
  - Click, Enter or Space opens it with focus inside, and it stays until
    Escape (which returns focus to the trigger), a click outside or the
    trigger again.
- **Embed**: `QuotedMessage` for pasted permalinks and forwards (#119): the
  author's avatar, name and time with a Jump link, "Originally posted in
  {channel}", a body clamped to four lines with "Show more", and an optional
  attachment strip. It takes `density` like the other embeds and is named
  "Quoted message from {author}".
- **Sortable**, a new component (#121): `Sortable`, `SortableGroup` and
  `SortableRow`.
  - Dragging a row shows a copy of it at reduced opacity and a line where it
    will land; an empty or collapsed group highlights and takes the row at
    its end.
  - With focus in a row, Alt+Shift+Up and Down move it one place, across
    groups at either end, and announce the new position.
  - `reorder_groups(true)` lets headers drag and take the same keys.
  - `on_reorder` reports a `SortableMove`; `SortableMove::apply` and
    `move_group` apply moves to your data. Nothing animates.
- **Accessibility audits** (#120): with the new `test-support` feature,
  `gpuicn::audit::unnamed_controls(window)` lists interactive elements with
  no accessible name in the last frame, and `A11yAuditOverlay` outlines them
  in the destructive color and logs their paths. Buttons, toggles,
  checkboxes, switches, selects, menus, tabs, inputs, the composer, sidebar
  rows and dialog triggers already registered; this release adds the
  remaining interactive parts.

## Changed

- **Menu** panels are as tall as the window allows before they scroll, on
  whichever side of the trigger has more room; context menus and submenus
  use the window height. Submenus open after 200ms of hover, and a sibling
  row closes them (#117).

## Upgrading

```sh
cargo install gpuicn-cli --version 0.9.4 --locked
gpuicn update --to 0.9.4
```

Crate users: `cargo update -p gpuicn`. For the audit, enable the feature in
your dev-dependencies: `gpuicn = { version = "0.9.4", features = ["test-support"] }`.
